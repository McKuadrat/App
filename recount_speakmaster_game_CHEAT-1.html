Recount Text Speaking Guide

1. Generic Structure (Struktur Teks)

1. Orientation

Memperkenalkan tokoh, tempat, dan waktu kejadian (*Who, Where, When*).

2. Events

Urutan peristiwa kronologis (*First, Next, Suddenly, After that*).

3. Re-orientation

Penutup cerita & kesan/perasaan pembicara (*I felt happy...*).

2. Top Irregular Past Verbs (Kata Kerja Lampau)

go ➔ went
see ➔ saw
eat ➔ ate
buy ➔ bought
take ➔ took
meet ➔ met
make ➔ made
feel ➔ felt

3. Time Connectives (Kata Hubung Urutan)

First / Firstly After that Then Suddenly A few moments later Finally
